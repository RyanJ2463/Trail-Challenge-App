IBM Plex Sans & Plex Mono

IBM's type team drew Plex to express "the relationship between mankind and machine" — the exact brief for an instrument app. Plex Sans for everything read as language: headings, body, labels. Plex Mono for everything read as a measurement: the hero numbers, pace splits, elevation, coordinates, timestamps. Monospace keeps digits in their column as a value ticks, and it reads as a trip computer, not a lifestyle feed.

SIL OFL · Google Fonts · one request · tabular figures via 'tnum'. Alt if Plex feels too corporate: Familjen Grotesk + a mono.
