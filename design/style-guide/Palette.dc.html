2 · COLOUR

Palette spec

Light-mode values. This direction runs on one accent and a cool grey ramp — colour is a signal, not decoration. Dark-mode shifts on the Modes board.

ACCENT

Slate#31527c · 49 82 124
The single brand colour: primary action, active nav, your route on the map, links, focus rings.
Cool, technical, unhurried — the blue of instrument dials and marine charts. Reads as "precision", not "outdoors". Use it on maybe 5% of any screen.
Slate deep#25405f · 37 64 95
Pressed states, and slate text that sits on the muted tint.
Slate muted#e9eef5 · 233 238 245
Selected-row background, quiet chips, progress-track, the info banner.
The accent at ~8% — enough to say "this is the active thing" without a fill.

NEUTRAL RAMP  — cool blue-grey, near-zero chroma

N-0 · Surface#ffffff
Cards, sheets, inputs. Large flat areas — the base layer the greys sit on.
N-50 · Background#f5f6f8 · 245 246 248
App background. A cool near-white — barely there, no warmth.
N-100 · Raised#eef0f3
Hover, pressed rows, secondary panels, table zebra.
N-200 · Border#e4e7ec · 228 231 236
Every hairline: card edges, dividers, 1px input outlines. This direction leans on borders, not shadows.
N-300 · Strong border#d3d7de · 211 215 222
Disabled surfaces, chart gridlines, elevation-profile axis.
N-400 · Faint#8a909c · 138 144 156
Muted icons, disabled controls, placeholder glyphs.
Not readable text — 3.0:1 on the background, fails AA. See the Accessibility board.
N-500 · Tertiary text#5f6673 · 95 102 115
Timestamps, split labels, coordinates, the least-important text.
~5:1 on light — the AA-safe floor for metadata.
N-600 · Secondary text#565e6c · 86 94 108
Body sub-text, captions, descriptions, unit labels.
6.0:1 — AA for every size.
N-900 · Ink#1a1d24 · 26 29 36
Headings and body copy. Cool blue-black, never pure #000.
15.6:1 — AAA everywhere.
