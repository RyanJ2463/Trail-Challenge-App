Why this system

Touchstones for "minimalist outdoor tech" — each choice traces to one of these.

14.2

Garmin / Suunto / Wahoo watch faces

A dark panel, one or two data fields huge, everything else tiny and grey. Colour appears only for a state or a route.

→ dark as a first-class mode, monospaced hero numbers, the single-accent rule, "the number is the interface".

swisstopo national maps

The gold standard of cartographic restraint — hairline contours on a near-white ground, one saturated colour reserved for the marked route.

→ the cool near-white background, contour-only base map with no terrain fill, slate as the one saturated thing on screen.

Aviation & marine instrument dials

Altimeters, chartplotters, depth sounders — legible in a glance under load, colour used strictly as signal: green is nominal, amber is caution, red is fault.

→ the disciplined semantic colours, high-contrast readouts, coordinates and headings set in mono.

Braun / Teenage Engineering / Dieter Rams

"As little design as possible." The object recedes; the function is the whole point; a single accent marks the one control that matters right now.

→ minimal chrome, borders not shadows, whitespace over decoration, colour spent only on the primary action.
Plex

IBM Plex & the IBM design language

Plex was drawn to sit "at the intersection of mankind and machine"; IBM's neutrals are a cool blue-grey, not a warm one.

→ the typeface itself, and the cool undertone running through every grey in the ramp.
